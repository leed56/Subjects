# Optional Sri Lankan grinding-mill catalogue

The grocery Stock page offers an owner-controlled import of 36 unbranded,
editable products. It does **not** run during shop creation. It skips SKUs
already present in the current shop and never updates those records.

The range includes chilli and other single-ingredient powders, raw and roasted
curry blends, Jaffna and meat/fish masalas, and rice, kurakkan, gram, undu and
atta flours. “Cummins” in the request is listed as **cumin**. The separate
images are original illustrations; they are not product or packaging photos.

Published Sri Lankan product ranges used to select this practical range:

- [Harischandra Mills — spices](https://www.harischandramills.com/product-category/spices/): chilli, turmeric, pepper, cinnamon, raw and roasted curry powders.
- [Harischandra Mills — flours](https://harischandramills.com/product-category/flour/): rice, kurakkan and ulundu flour, with examples of real pack sizes.
- [Wijaya Products — spices](https://spiceswijay.com/our-products/spices-in-sri-lanka/): cumin, roasted chilli, Jaffna curry, meat/fish blends and masalas.
- [Wijaya Products — flours](https://spiceswijay.com/our-products/flour/): red/white rice, kurakkan, gram, undu and atta flour, plus thosai mix.
- [Derana Foods — spices](https://deranafoods.lk/chili-spices/): coriander, fennel, fenugreek, goraka and mustard products.
- [Mallika Grinding Mills, via Industrial Development Board](https://bizconnect.idb.gov.lk/listing/nuwara-eliya/food-and-beverages/mallika-grinding-mills/): local mill examples including chicken masala and sambar podi.

Pack sizes in the template are **editable starting examples**, not claims
about a particular brand. Every imported record starts **inactive** with
zero stock and zero cost/selling price. Barcodes are blank because they cannot
be invented. Owners must verify recipes/ingredient labels, allergen and
expiry information, pack size, physical quantity, supplier, cost, retail price
and barcode before activating a product. Translations are display aliases tied
to stable SKUs, so editing a product's name preserves the owner's wording.

The scope is stocked grocery products. Customer-owned ingredient grinding,
batch transformation, raw-material yield and fee-per-kg services need a
separate production workflow; this catalogue does not pretend that a retail
product sale covers those operations.
