# Optional Sri Lankan grocery meat and poultry range

The Stock page offers a second, owner-controlled import with nine unbranded
templates. The existing starter **Chicken** product stays untouched. No shop
receives these products during creation or deployment. A confirmed import adds
only missing template SKUs to the current grocery shop. A retry does not reset
an owner's names, prices, stock or images.

| Template | Starting selling unit |
| --- | --- |
| Chicken Breast | 1 kg |
| Chicken Drumsticks | 1 kg |
| Mixed Chicken Parts | 1 kg |
| Chicken Sausages (Small Pack) | 80 g |
| Chicken Sausages (Large Pack) | 500 g |
| Chicken Meatballs | 200 g |
| Whole Broiler Chicken | 1 kg |
| Broiler Parent Chicken | 1 kg |
| Farm Chicken | 1 kg |

These names and distinctions follow Sri Lankan published ranges: [Crysbro's
breast, drumstick and mixed part packs](https://www.crysbro.com/productpage/?range=bulk),
[Delmo's whole broiler, sausage and meatball range](https://delmo.lk/products),
and [MeatMart's parent and farm chicken listings](https://meatmart.lk/products/chicken/).
Pack sizes are editable starting examples, not representations of a brand.
Nine separate original SVG illustrations are included; they are not supplier
photos or copied packaging.

Each record starts inactive, with zero price, zero cost, zero stock and no
barcode. Before activation the owner must verify the cut, chilled/frozen
handling, actual pack or weighing unit, expiry and storage information, supplier,
cost, selling price, barcode and physical received quantity. This is a product
range, not a food safety certification. The Sinhala and Tamil display aliases
are drafts pending the user's native reviewers.
